How ranking works

Recommended combines three things the index can show. Nobody can pay for rank, and no publisher's packs get a boost.

recommended = 0.4 × trust + 0.3 × freshness + 0.3 × popularity
  trust      = 1.0 reviewed · 0.6 listed, every profile passed · 0.3 otherwise
  freshness  = 1.0 if checked in the last 30 days, falling to 0 at 365 days
  popularity = min(1, log10(1 + stars) ÷ 3)        1,000 stars = 1.0
with a search: relevance ÷ best relevance × (0.7 + 0.3 × recommended)
ties: name

Search matches names first, then categories, keywords, stack, and profiles, then descriptions. It accepts the start of a word and one typo in words of five letters or more, and expands a few common words, such as rest to web and FastAPI.
